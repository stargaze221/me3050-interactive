3. Read the characteristic frequency

1/τ

First-order corner frequency

Near ω = 1/τ, the response begins its transition toward the −20 dB/decade high-frequency slope and phase lag approaches −45°.

ωₙ

Second-order natural frequency

Near ωₙ, damping determines whether the magnitude has a resonant peak and how rapidly phase changes toward −180°.

jω

Frequency response is the transfer function on the imaginary axis

Evaluating G(s) at s = jω directly gives the sinusoidal steady-state magnitude ratio and phase shift.
