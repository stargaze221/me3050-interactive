3. Why is critical damping special?

1
Below c = 4

The poles are complex conjugates. The response can return quickly, but it oscillates around equilibrium.

2
At c = 4

The poles meet at s = −2. This is the boundary between oscillatory and non-oscillatory motion.

3
Above c = 4

The poles split along the real axis. One pole moves back toward the imaginary axis, producing a slower mode.

Critical damping is the fastest non-oscillatory return to equilibrium. Watch the dashed critical-response curve while moving c above 4: extra damping actually makes the slow mode slower.
