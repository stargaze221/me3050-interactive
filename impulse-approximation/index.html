1. Shrinking-width pulse with fixed area

As Δ decreases, the pulse gets narrower and taller. If the area stays fixed at A, the pulse approximates Aδ(t−τ).

Impulse-response formula
The ideal impulse response of T ẏ + y = u is h(t) = 1T e−t/T u(t). A shifted weighted impulse Aδ(t−τ) therefore produces Ah(t−τ).

Impulse response building block

In an LTI system, each weighted shifted impulse produces a weighted shifted copy of the impulse response.
